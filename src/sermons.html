First-Time Online Guests

What to Expect During the Broadcast

Whether you are joining us for the first time or worship every week from afar, here is how you can engage fully with our Sunday gathering.

01

10:25 AM Prelude

The broadcast begins 5 minutes before service with pre-service music and live chat greetings in the virtual foyer.

02

Inspiring Praise

Worship through beloved hymns and modern praise songs led by Worship Pastor Rev. Dr. Lisa Moore and choir.

03

Biblical Preaching

Hear chapter-by-chapter expository messages taught by Interim Pastor Kirk Bookout with life applications.

04

Live Intercession

Submit your prayer requests during or after the stream to be lifted up by Associate Pastor Rev. Becky Fauntleroy.
