Church of God World Missions • Continental Outreach Field Update

Africa Mission Fund

“Walking alongside African national churches and congregations of the Church of God.”

Working through Regional Coordinators of the Church of God for the African continent, the Africa Mission Fund enables coordinators to walk alongside national churches to provide timely emergency funding and support developing ministry strategies. For example, our church family's giving recently assisted two pastors and their congregations in Burundi who lost everything in severe flooding.
